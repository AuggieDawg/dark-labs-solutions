# Dark Labs Brand Foundation

This document is the source of truth for Dark Labs positioning, public voice,
sales language, and delivery promises. The website should sound like the same
person a client meets: direct, observant, ambitious, honest about uncertainty,
and accountable when the work becomes difficult.

## Mission

Dark Labs helps established businesses grow on solid digital ground by planning
and building dependable, measurable customer-acquisition systems—and surfacing
foreseeable risks early, while they are less expensive to address.

## Vision

Build Dark Labs into the most trusted founder-led business technology firm in
the Uintah Basin, then become a nationally respected builder of durable,
measurable digital systems.

## Client promise

Dark Labs plans ahead, exposes risks early, communicates costs honestly, builds
carefully, measures what happens after launch, and leaves each client with a
system whose ownership, access, costs, and handoff responsibilities are clear.

## Founder standard

> My name and reputation are attached to what I build. I will tell you what I
> know, identify what still needs to be verified, find the answer, explain the
> tradeoff, and remain accountable for the work Dark Labs controls.

The reputation Dark Labs intends to earn is:

> Go with Dark Labs. Agustin will tell you the truth, find the answer, and follow
> through when the work gets difficult.

That sentence is an aspiration, not an entitlement. It must be earned through
disciplined planning, careful execution, clear boundaries, and follow-through
during the agreed engagement and stabilization period.

## Operating creed

1. Plan deeply.
2. Tell the truth early.
3. Build responsibly.
4. Measure honestly.
5. Protect the client.
6. Stay accountable.
7. Earn the referral.

## North-star outcome: a reference-worthy system

A Dark Labs project is successful when the system is:

- **Stable:** the critical customer, lead, notification, integration, and
  recovery paths included in the engagement have been tested in the real
  environment.
- **Measurable:** meaningful customer actions are captured well enough to guide
  the next decision.
- **Documented:** ownership, dependencies, recurring costs, operating steps,
  and support boundaries are clear.
- **Ownership-defined:** account ownership, client access, recurring costs, and
  handoff responsibilities are documented for the agreed system.
- **Commercially sound:** known third-party costs are disclosed and scope
  changes are tracked.
- **Recommendable:** after the system has operated in the real business, the
  client would confidently hire or recommend Dark Labs again.

## Voice

Dark Labs copy should:

- lead with the business problem and customer outcome;
- sound founder-led without making every section about the founder;
- use plain language before technical terminology;
- communicate conviction about the process without pretending certainty;
- name risks, ownership, costs, dependencies, and follow-through;
- show the mechanism behind claims instead of relying on words such as
  “premium,” “innovative,” or “cutting-edge”;
- prefer proof, client approval, and verified measurement over inflated
  performance claims.

Dark Labs can adjust the energy and sequence of a conversation to the client.
Relationship-driven owners may respond to personal commitment and shared
ambition. Analytical owners may need architecture, evidence, risks, and costs
first. The delivery changes; the truth does not.

## Claims boundary

Dark Labs can promise the quality of the process and workmanship it controls:
planning, communication, implementation, testing, documentation, ownership,
measurement, launch discipline, and agreed support.

Dark Labs does not guarantee revenue, lead volume, search rankings, market
demand, third-party uptime, or client sales performance. When an outcome depends
on factors outside Dark Labs' control, the dependency must be stated clearly.

## Brand line

**Technology behind the scenes. Your business in the spotlight.**
